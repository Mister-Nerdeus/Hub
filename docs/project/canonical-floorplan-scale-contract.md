# Canonical Floorplan Scale Contract

Plan 1 uses feet as the geometry unit. The base room/bed module is 10 ft x 10 ft.

Allowable exceptions are explicit:

- Level 1 Trauma may use a larger footprint.
- Provider/pharmacy and nurse station support zones may use support-area footprints.
- Candidate split-bay bed positions remain 10 ft x 10 ft positions inside a paired bay model.

Area alone does not create simulation capacity, ratio eligibility, assignment eligibility, or room-load eligibility.
