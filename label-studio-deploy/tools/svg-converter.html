Profile notes

Background: 540 × 215 is an artwork size, not the physical label size. Full label: 591 × 827 represents approximately 50 × 70 mm at 300 DPI. Printer crop: first renders 591 × 827, then removes 7 pixels on the left and 8 on the right without resampling.

Conversion runs locally. Original SVG source is not uploaded. The two-color indexed PNG is encoded at 1 bit per pixel; a three-color indexed PNG uses 2 bits per pixel. Browser color rendering and physical red registration still require testing with the exact printer and media.
